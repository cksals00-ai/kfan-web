The trip checklist

15 things worth doing before you fly, in the order they matter. The app version works offline and remembers what you've ticked.

Share Post on X Facebook

D-60 · Tickets

  1. Make your ticketing account earlyKorean ticket sites need a verified account, and verification for a foreign passport can take days. Do this weeks before, not the night before.
  2. Match the name exactlyThe booking name must match the passport you will carry. Mismatched names are the most common reason fans are refused at pickup.
  3. Work out the sale time in your timezoneSales open in Korea Standard Time (UTC+9). Convert it once, write it down, set two alarms.
  4. Never buy from a reseller DMResold tickets are frequently cancelled at the door and scams targeting overseas fans are common. Official channels only.

D-30 · Flights & stay

  1. Book the room before the crowdHotels near a venue rise in price once a tour date is confirmed. Book refundable early rather than cheap late.
  2. Check your return flight against the showAn early-morning flight after a late show means no sleep and a rushed exit. Give yourself a buffer day if you can.
  3. Pick your base by the venueUse the venue guide's 'Where to stay' section. Being 40 minutes away costs you the whole post-show evening.

D-7 · Entry & money

  1. Check entry requirements for your passportKorea's electronic travel authorisation (K-ETA) applies to some nationalities and not others, and the rules change. Check the official government site for your country now.
  2. Sort data before you landAn eSIM or a pocket wifi router. You will need maps and messages from the moment you arrive.
  3. Get a transit cardA rechargeable transit card works on subway, bus and in convenience stores. Buy it at any convenience store on arrival.
  4. Carry some cashCards work almost everywhere, but merch booths, small cafes and markets sometimes do not.

Show day

  1. Drop your luggage firstNo Korean venue stores suitcases. Hotel, station locker, or luggage service — before you go anywhere near the venue.
  2. Eat before you arriveVenue food is thin and queues are long. Check the venue guide for where the food actually is.
  3. Bring: passport, ticket, lightstick, batteries, waterPassport for pickup and ID checks. Spare batteries because vendors outside charge a premium.
  4. Decide your exit before the encoreKnow your last train time and your backup. Deciding while 18,000 people move around you is how trips end badly.
