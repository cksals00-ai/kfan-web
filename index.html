Venue guides

Gocheok Sky Dome

Korea's only domed stadium — rain never cancels, but the air gets thick.

KSPO Dome

The classic K-pop room — inside Olympic Park, still called 'Cheju' by locals.

Inspire Arena

Korea's first purpose-built arena — and it's attached to your hotel, next to the airport.

The intimate one — smallest of the five, and everything else is right outside.

Korea's first arena built purely for music — opening in Changdong, north Seoul.
