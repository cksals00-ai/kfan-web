Gocheok Sky Dome

고척스카이돔

Korea's only domed stadium — rain never cancels, but the air gets thick.

18,076 seats · Guro-gu, Seoul · Opened 2015

Share Post on X Facebook

Getting there

  • Line 1 to Guil Station, Exit 3 — a 5-minute walk. This is the fastest route from central Seoul.
  • Gocheok Station (Line 1) is closer on the map but exits on the wrong side of the road; Guil is the local habit.
  • From Incheon Airport allow 90 minutes. There is no direct airport bus to the dome.
  • Taxis after a show queue for a very long time — see 'Getting out'.
Most people get this wrongLine 1 is the oldest line and runs above ground here. It is slower than the map suggests on concert days — add 20 minutes.

Bags & storage

  • Coin lockers sit inside the dome concourse and fill up 3+ hours before doors. Small sizes go first.
  • Guil Station has a second bank of lockers — a common backup when the dome is full.
  • Large suitcases are not accepted in either location. Do not come straight from the airport with luggage.
  • Rules on bag size at the door are enforced inconsistently; a tote and a small backpack are always fine.
Most people get this wrongIf you land on show day, drop luggage at your hotel or at a Seoul Station luggage service first. Arriving with a suitcase is the single most common ruined-evening story.

Lightsticks & what you can bring

  • Official lightsticks are welcome. Bring spare batteries — vendors outside charge a premium.
  • Professional cameras with detachable lenses are refused at the door. Phones are fine.
  • Outside food and drink are generally refused; sealed water is usually allowed.
  • Banners are allowed but staff may ask you to lower anything that blocks the view behind you.

Merch queue

  • The merch booth opens hours before doors and the queue forms outside, exposed to weather.
  • Popular items sell out on day one of a multi-night run. Day two and three are noticeably quieter.
  • Queue positions are usually handled by staff-issued order, not by arriving at dawn — check the venue's own notice before camping overnight.
  • Card payment is standard; cash-only booths are rare now.
Most people get this wrongIf your seat is high up, buy merch AFTER the show on a multi-night run — the post-show queue on the final night is often shorter than the pre-show one.

Where you'll be sitting

  • Floor sections are flat, so anyone in front of you blocks the stage. Rows past the mid-floor mark see less than a raised 1st-tier seat.
  • 1st tier along the sides gives the best value view — full stage plus the crowd.
  • The upper ring behind the stage is sold as restricted view and it truly is; you will watch the screens.
  • Sound is boomy under the dome roof. Seats near the rear upper ring get noticeable echo.

Food, water, toilets

  • Concourse food is limited stadium fare. Eat before you arrive.
  • Convenience stores cluster around Guil Station exit — the practical pre-show stop.
  • Toilet queues at the 1st-tier concourse are the longest; walk one level up or down.
  • The dome gets warm and humid with a full house. Carry water in.

Getting out & last trains

  • Roughly 18,000 people leave at once into a residential street. Expect 20–30 minutes just to clear the exits.
  • Line 1's last train is generally around midnight; shows finishing past 22:00 make this tight if there is an encore.
  • Ride-hailing surge pricing is severe here and drivers avoid the area during the crush.
  • Walking 10 minutes away from the dome before calling a car is the standard local move.
Most people get this wrongCheck your line's last train the day before — the last train time is posted at the station, and it differs by direction.

Where to stay

  • Guro / Sindorim: closest cluster, business hotels, walkable or one stop away.
  • Hongdae or Myeongdong: 40–50 minutes by Line 1, far more to do before and after.
  • Staying near the dome is quiet at night — good for sleep, thin on food after a late show.
  • Airport-day fans often stay near Seoul Station for the direct AREX link.
