Jamsil Indoor Arena

잠실실내체육관

The intimate one — smallest of the five, and everything else is right outside.

11,069 seats · Songpa-gu, Seoul · Opened 1979

Share Post on X Facebook

Getting there

  • Line 2 to Sports Complex Station, Exit 6 — a short walk.
  • Line 9 also serves Sports Complex Station; Line 9's express skips some stations, check before boarding.
  • The Sports Complex hosts several venues — confirm which building your show is in.
  • Central location means normal taxi fares, unlike Gocheok or Inspire.
Most people get this wrongThe Jamsil complex contains the Indoor Arena, the Baseball Stadium and the Olympic Stadium. Fans regularly walk to the wrong one. Check the building name on your ticket.

Bags & storage

  • Station lockers at Sports Complex and nearby Jamsil Station are the practical option.
  • Jamsil Station connects to a huge mall with more storage and everything else.
  • Venue lockers are few — this is an old building.
  • Same rule on suitcases.

Lightsticks & what you can bring

  • Standard rules: official lightstick yes, professional camera no.
  • The room is small enough that lightstick waves actually read from every seat.
  • Sealed water generally fine.
  • Older building — power outlets for charging are scarce inside.

Merch queue

  • Booths set up outside the building with limited shelter.
  • The mall at Jamsil Station is five minutes away if you need to wait somewhere warm or cool first.
  • Smaller capacity means smaller crowds than KSPO or Gocheok.
  • Sellouts still happen on popular runs.

Where you'll be sitting

  • The smallest of the five — even the back row is genuinely close.
  • Old-building sightlines: some seats have pillar or angle issues. Restricted-view labels are worth believing.
  • Steep upper sections give a full view of the floor formations.
  • Sound is decent but this is not a purpose-built concert hall.
Most people get this wrongIf you want to actually see faces rather than screens, this is the room to pick.

Food, water, toilets

  • Jamsil is dense with restaurants, cafes and convenience stores — the best surrounding area of the five.
  • The mall food court handles a pre-show meal easily.
  • Venue toilets are dated and queue quickly; use the mall before you walk over.
  • Everything you forgot to pack can be bought within 10 minutes here.

Getting out & last trains

  • 11,000 people is a manageable crowd — the fastest exit of the five.
  • Lines 2 and 9 both serve the station, so you have options if one is jammed.
  • Line 2 is a loop line running late; still confirm the last train in your direction.
  • Taxis are readily available on the main road — a real advantage here.

Where to stay

  • Jamsil itself: walkable to the venue, hotels at every price point, mall and river park.
  • Gangnam: 15 minutes on Line 2.
  • This is the best 'stay where the show is' area in Seoul for a fan trip.
  • If your itinerary has shows at both Jamsil and KSPO, this base covers both.
