Seoul Arena

서울아레나

Korea's first arena built purely for music — opening in Changdong, north Seoul.

18,269 seats · Changdong, Dobong-gu, Seoul · Opening 2027

Share Post on X Facebook

Status

  • Under construction in Changdong, northern Seoul, with an announced capacity around 18,000.
  • Designed as a dedicated music arena rather than a converted sports hall.
  • Opening was announced for 2027; dates have moved before and may move again.
  • This page will be filled in with real bag, queue and exit details once the venue opens and the first shows run.
Most people get this wrongWe will not invent details for a building nobody has queued outside yet. Update this app after opening for the full guide.

Getting there (planned)

  • Changdong is served by Line 1 and Line 4 — expect Changdong Station to be the main access point.
  • Northern Seoul: allow more travel time from Gangnam or Jamsil than for central venues.
  • The GTX-C line is planned to serve this area in future, which would change access substantially.
  • Confirm official access guidance when the venue publishes it.

Where to stay (early view)

  • Hotel supply in Changdong is thin today compared with Jamsil or Hongdae.
  • Nodeul / Nowon and along Line 4 are the nearest practical clusters.
  • Staying central and travelling out is likely to be the common pattern at first.
  • Expect prices near the venue to move sharply once a major tour is announced.
