Opening 2027

Korea's first arena built specifically for K-pop performances, currently under construction.

18,000+ seats

Roughly the scale of Gocheok Sky Dome, purpose-designed for concerts rather than sports.

Changdong, northern Seoul

Dobong-gu, on the subway network's northeastern corridor — a new direction for concert trips, away from the usual Gangnam/Songpa axis.
